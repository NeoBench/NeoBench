NeoBench applications
======================

Programs do not live here yet: everything the desktop can open is
linked into the ROM image and started by the kernel, so there is
nothing to install and nothing to launch from a path.

The program entries that do exist are filed where they belong rather
than here: Config/Preferences starts the preferences pane, Tools/ holds
the clock, the monitor and the reader, and Core/Docs holds About --
each standing beside the files it goes with.  This directory is kept
for the ones that arrive as files of their own -- an archive unpacked
into the store, or a fetch that landed in Temp/ and was moved here
afterwards.  Until one does, it holds this note and nothing else.

  Clock, Monitor, NeoText    filed in Tools/, opened from there
  About                      filed in Core/Docs, also on the start menu
  Preferences                filed in Config, taken off the menu
  Files                      the drawer that leads the start menu
  the rest                   not written yet
