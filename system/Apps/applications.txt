NeoBench applications
======================

Programs do not live here yet: everything the desktop can open is
linked into the ROM image and started by the kernel, so there is
nothing to install and nothing to launch from a path.

The program entries that do exist are filed where they belong rather
than here: Config/Preferences starts the preferences pane, Tools/ holds
the clock, the monitor, the reader and the shell, Core/Docs holds About
and Core/Media holds VLC -- each standing beside the files it goes with.
This directory is kept for the ones that arrive as files of their own
-- an archive unpacked into the store, or a fetch that landed in Temp/
and was moved here afterwards.  Until one does, it holds this note and
nothing else.

  Clock, Monitor, NeoText, NeoShell    filed in Tools/, opened from there
  About                      filed in Core/Docs, off the start menu
  Preferences                filed in Config, first row below the rule
  VLC                        filed in Core/Media, the row after it
  Files                      the drawer that leads the start menu
  the rest                   not written yet
