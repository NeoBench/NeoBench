NeoBench applications
======================

Programs do not live here yet: everything the desktop can open is
linked into the ROM image and started by the kernel, so there is
nothing to install and nothing to launch from a path.

The two program entries that do exist are filed where they belong
rather than here: Config/Preferences starts the preferences pane and
Tools/NeoText starts the reader, each standing beside the files it
works with.  This directory is kept for the ones that arrive as files
of their own -- an archive unpacked into the store, or a fetch that
landed in Temp/ and was moved here afterwards.  Until one does, it
holds this note and nothing else.

  Files, Clock, Monitor, About   built in, opened from the start menu
  NeoText, Preferences           filed in Tools/ and Config/
  the rest                       not written yet
