NeoBench Music
==============

Audio goes here.

VLC plays the sounds in this directory, and the ones filed with the
system in Core/Media appear in the same list: the store is the
whole of what the player can see, and it walks these four drawers
in turn rather than the one it happens to be standing in.

Two kinds are read.  RIFF wave files of eight or sixteen bits, one
channel or two, and NeoBench's own NSND -- which is what the store
ships in Core/Media, and what the sound the kernel makes arrives
as.  Either way the samples end up in the chip RAM Paula reads,
stereo down to the one channel Paula has, and the right hand
brought under the left on the way.

The sound runs from the head again after it has been stopped.  That
is not a limit the player would choose: Paula's position in a
sample is not something that can be read back, so there is nothing
to come back to.  What is true of the restart is that it is the
whole file again, at the volume the desktop's preferences carry.

arpeggio.wav is here as an example: four notes, the right channel
held at half of the left, so a machine listening for the downmix
can hear the difference.

The startup chime does not live in this directory.  It ships in
Core/Media as startup.snd and is played by the kernel, because it
has to be there before there is a store to look in and a sound
card to ask about it.
