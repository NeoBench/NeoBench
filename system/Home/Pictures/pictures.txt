NeoBench Pictures
=================

Still images go here.

VLC opens one of these on its own pane: the file is read, the pixels
it carries are put into the pane, and the next name in the list
takes the same route when it is chosen.  Four kinds are read as they
stand, and there is one of each in this directory as an example:

  spectrum.ppm    portable pixmap, P6, three samples a byte
  tiles.bmp       Windows bitmap, eight bits and a palette
  plasma.png      network graphic, zlib and all five row filters
  rings.iff       IFF ILBM, eight planes and ByteRun1

Anything else is reported as what it is rather than guessed at, and
the message under the transport says so in as many words.

The wallpaper is not a picture and is not in this directory: the
desktop draws its own backdrop out of a gradient, three glows, a
hairline grid and the NeoBench mark, all of it composited in the
ROM rather than read from a file.
