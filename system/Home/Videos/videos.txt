NeoBench Videos
===============

Moving pictures go here.

VLC plays these.  The kind it reads is YUV4MPEG2: one header line
saying how wide and how tall the picture is and how many frames a
second it is cut for, then a frame at a time of luma and of the two
chroma samples that carry the colour beside it.  Each frame is
turned into red, green and blue for the pane as it is shown, and
the player steps on to the next one on the field count rather than
on a clock of its own.

Space starts it and stops it, and so does Return.  The left and
right arrows walk the frames one at a time, which is the way to
look at what a particular frame is doing: they stop the film to do
it.  The buttons under the list are the same four things -- play
and pause, stop, and the way to the entry before or the one after.

orbit.y4m is here as an example.  It is a lamp going round a mark,
five frames of it, and it is about all the moving picture the store
carries: a film is the one kind of media here whose size is nothing
to do with how well it is written.
