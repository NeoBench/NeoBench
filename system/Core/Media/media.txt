NeoBench media
==============

The desktop is composited at run time: there are no bitmaps in the
ROM image.  Every mark, tile and glyph on screen is built from the
gfx primitives in boot/rom/gfx.c and quantised into the AGA palette
by a median cut over the finished back buffer.

  screen      hires 640x512, interlaced, eight bitplanes
  colours     64, produced per frame rather than chosen by hand
  font        Xen, nine pixels, a reconstruction of the Amiga face
  logo        vector, drawn from the same primitives as the icons

Wallpaper colours, the grid and the horizon glows are all preference
values -- see Config/screen.cfg.
