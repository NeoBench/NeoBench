NeoBench bench
==============

A NeoBench-specific benchmark harness, not a port of anything written
for AmigaDOS.  Everything here measures the machine directly:

  mips        integer loop against the 68060 at the detected clock
  fpu         double precision add/mul/div through the FPU probe path
  mem         sequential and random access across fast RAM
  chip        chip RAM bandwidth under eight-plane fetch
  blit        chipset blitter fill and copy, 32-bit fetch
  gfx         back-buffer composite and median-cut present, the cost
              the desktop pays on every repaint

Results are reported in the boot log style: [  OK  ] against the
reference machine, [ WARN ] when the board is slow, [ FAILED ] when
the measurement could not be taken at all.
